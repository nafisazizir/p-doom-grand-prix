## The course (v2/src/course.js) — what's around your sections
The road now turns, climbs, banks; it's ±9 wide (kerbs to ±10.3). `layout(zf, rng, sec)` placements: `x` = lateral offset (world units) from the centre line, `y` = height above local ground (terrain height is computed for you; props sit on the hills), objects auto-rotate to face the oncoming driver (+z = towards the camera) with `rotY` added. Engine placement rules (in `v2/src/track.js` `_spawn`):
- ground props over water / void are dropped (bridges, cliff side, space gaps); overhead props (y ≥ 6) always placed relative to the road.
- in tunnels: props with |x| > 17.5 are dropped, overhead props with y > 15 dropped (tunnel interior half-width ≈ 19, ceiling ≈ 17).
- during jumps nothing spawns. In space/rainbow sections everything floats at road height (that's fine: it's space).
Features per section: s1 garage: start grid. s2 chatbot: elevated highway 18.2–22.4 s (ground props sit on the ground below; tall props look great). s3 shoggoth: bridge over purple glowing swamp 28.6–33.2 s. s4 datacenter: tunnel 37.9–55.5 s. s5 sydney: pink suspension bridge over the sea (whole section). s6 latent: rainbow road in space. s7 badlands: glide jump from space 73.7–77.5 s, then desert; a hairpin left turn 80.4–83.3 s. s8 badlands (sec.escalate): cliff edge — left side is a sheer drop to the ocean (left ground props dropped). s9 paperclip: normal ground. s10 datacenter-fire: tunnel (almost whole section). s11 stadium: banked oval, small hop jump 133.9–135.1 s. s12 takeoff: steep exponential ramp, space from 150 s.
Use distant landmarks (|x| 60–300, big scale) for skyline/mountains/stadium/factories so the world feels big; they sit on the terrain hills.
